# 🍄 Spores & Shadows

**Fabric 1.21.1** | **1,400+ Blocks** | **Multi-Language (EN/IT/DE/ES/FR)**

> In vanilla Minecraft, wood lasts forever. **Not anymore.** 
> Darkness, humidity, and subterranean air rot your structures and release lethal toxic miasma. Will your timber kingdom survive?

---

### ✨ Key Features

- 🪵 **Dynamic Wood Decay**: Over 1,400 wooden block variants naturally age through 4 stages (*Healthy ➔ Tainted ➔ Moldy ➔ Rotten*).
- ☠️ **Toxic Miasma**: Stagnant, dark, or flooded underground rooms accumulate choking spores causing nausea and poisoning.
- 🪟 **Ventilation System**: Keep air clean with open windows, iron bars, copper grates, or dedicated chimneys.
- 🏭 **Steampunk Machines**: Build **Dehumidifiers** to dry the air and **Air Purifiers** with replaceable filters to neutralize gas.
- 📡 **Redstone Automation**: Wall and ceiling sensors detect rot and spores to power automated fan networks.
- 🐝 **Preserve & Restore**: Coat critical beams with **Honeycomb** to make them decay-proof, or scrape mold away using an axe.
- 🖥️ **Total World Immersion**: Rotten GUIs, failing redstone components, detuned instruments, and pre-aged vanilla structures.

---

### 📦 Requirements & Compatibility

- **Required**: [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/) (0.15+), [Fabric API](https://curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/fabric-api), [Cloth Config](https://curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/cloth-config), [Polymer](https://curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/polymer).
- **Supports**: Jade / WTHIT (HUD overlays), JEI (recipes), Sodium/Iris (shaders), and standard Tech/Energy cables (FE).
