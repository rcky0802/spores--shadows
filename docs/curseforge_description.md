> **In vanilla Minecraft, wood is immortal.**  
> You build a wooden cabin, dig a mine with timber supports, and they stay pristine forever.  
> **Not anymore.**  
>
> **Spores & Shadows** turns wood into a living, vulnerable material. Darkness and dampness breed virulent mold, structures age with time, and enclosed subterranean rooms fill with choking, toxic miasma.  
> *Will your wooden kingdom endure, or will you succumb to the spores?*

---

## 🔥 Why Play Spores & Shadows?

### 🪵 1. Your Builds Finally Feel Alive
Wooden structures naturally progress through **4 visual decay stages**: *Healthy ➔ Tainted ➔ Moldy ➔ Rotten*.  
- **Over 1,400 block variants** covering every single wood species in the game.
- Sunshine, torchlight, and dry mountain peaks preserve your timber.
- Deep damp cellars, rain, and stagnant pools breed voracious rot.

---

### ☠️ 2. The Air Becomes a Hazard
Neglected wooden basements and damp mineshafts don't just rot—they exhale **toxic airborne miasma**.
- In sealed rooms, spore density rises from eerie whispers to **starvation, violent nausea, and lethal poisoning**.
- **Ventilation matters!** Crack open a window, install iron bars or copper grates, or carve a chimney to let fresh air flow.
- Strap on a **3D Steampunk Spore Mask** or enchant your helmet with **Spore Filtration** to brave deep, forgotten ruins.

---

### 🏭 3. Steampunk Climate Engineering
Don't surrender your home to the fungus—fight back with industrial machinery:
- 🌀 **The Dehumidifier**: Dries room air to 0% humidity, condenses moisture into harvestable water, and prevents rot before it starts.
- 🌬️ **The Air Purifier**: High-output air scrubber that burns through replaceable filter cartridges to cleanse deadly toxic air in seconds.
- 📡 **Wall & Ceiling Sensors**: Mount smart **Moisture & Spore Detectors** on walls or ceilings to trigger automated ventilation fans with direct Redstone signals!

---

### 🪓 4. Care, Restore & Reclaim
- 🐝 **Honeycomb Waxing**: Coat wooden beams in beeswax to permanently freeze their state and make them 100% immune to decay and miasma.
- 🪓 **Axe Scraping**: `Shift + Right Click` with an axe to scrape away surface mold and restore damaged wood.
- 📐 **Planks Recovery**: Reclaim moldy planks on your crafting table to wash out the spores and salvage clean lumber!

---

### 🎭 5. Decay in Every Detail
The rot isn't just a skin-deep texture—it infects the whole world:
- 🖥️ **Rotting GUIs**: Open an infested crafting table, chest, or barrel to see mold and moss crawling across the container screen!
- 💥 **Mechanical Breakdown**: Rotten wooden buttons and pressure plates stick active for seconds; trapped chests misfire; decaying bookshelves lose enchanting power.
- 🎶 **Haunting Audio**: Infested note blocks sound muffled and off-pitch, while rotten jukeboxes play warped, sluggish vinyl records.
- 🏛️ **Weathered Ruins**: Shipwrecks, mineshafts, and witch huts naturally spawn pre-aged to match their environment.

---

## 🧭 Survival in 3 Simple Steps

1. **Light & Dry**: Keep wooden rooms well-lit and away from stagnant water pools.
2. **Wax What You Love**: Always carry a few honeycombs to seal critical roof beams and treasured chests.
3. **Breathe Easy**: If you're mining deep underground, watch for spore clouds, carry a Spore Mask, and always leave a ventilation shaft!

## 📦 Mod Requirements & Compatibility

### 🔴 Mandatory Dependencies (Must Install):
* **[Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/)**: `0.15.0+` *(Tested: `0.19.3` for Minecraft 1.21.1, Java 21)*
* **[Fabric API](https://curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/fabric-api)**: `0.100.0+` *(Tested: `0.116.15+1.21.1`)*
* **[Cloth Config v15](https://curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/cloth-config)**: `15.0.128+`
* **[Polymer (Core & Resource Pack)](https://curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/polymer)**: `0.9.19+` *(Handles client models and virtual textures)*

### 🟢 Recommended & Tested Mods (Optional):
* **[Mod Menu](https://curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/modmenu)** (`11.0.1+`): Access in-game configuration screens directly from the pause menu.
* **[Jade](https://curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/jade)** (`15.10.6+`) or **[WTHIT](https://curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/wthit)** (`12.4.0+`): Displays live decay stage, waxing status, and infection risk % on your HUD.
* **[JEI (Just Enough Items)](https://curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/jei)** (`19.21.0+`): Visual recipes for waxing, scraping, planks recovery, and machines.
* **[Sodium](https://curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/sodium)** (`0.6.0+`) & **[Iris](https://curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/irisshaders)** (`1.8.0+`): Performance and shaders. Fully tested with zero visual flickering (Z-fighting).
* **Tech / Energy Mods** (*Tech Reborn*, *Modern Industrialization*, etc.): Power Dehumidifiers and Purifiers with FE / Energy cables.
* **Fluid Piping Mods** (*Any mod supporting Fabric Transfer API*): Automate water extraction from Dehumidifiers.
